About Tembisa & Midrand Rentals

Trusted Rooms. Real People. Real Value.

Tembisa & Midrand Rentals is a dedicated rental platform for Tembisa and Midrand — powered by Vusani Ikhaya Properties (Pty) Ltd. We connect tenants with landlords and agents across every Tembisa section and every Midrand suburb.

Our listings cover all Tembisa sections, extensions 1–27, Winnie Mandela extensions, neighbouring township areas (Ivory Park, Ebony Park, Rabie Ridge, Kaalfontein, Mayibuye, Clayville, Olifantsfontein, Klipfontein View) and Midrand's full suburb, estate and agricultural holding network — including Waterfall City, Dainfern, Steyn City, Kyalami, Carlswald and more.

To make finding a rental in Tembisa and Midrand simple, transparent and safe — with verified listings, real contacts and clear pricing.
